What’s left after the bills?

Suppose ₹80,000 in salary comes in. You spend ₹1,250 on groceries and have a ₹900 internet bill due. Home shows ₹77,850 left after bills. Pay the internet bill and it moves from due to spent. Your remaining amount stays ₹77,850.

FinVision · Savings goals

Will your monthly deposit get you there?

For a ₹1,20,000 school-fees goal, twelve ₹5,000 deposits at an estimated 7% a year project to ₹62,310.66. That includes ₹60,000 of deposits and ₹2,310.66 of estimated interest. With no other savings linked, the gap is ₹57,689.34.

Choose a deadline, link the savings already set aside, and see the balance along the way. Change the term or rate to compare the outcome. If you have an existing FinVision plan, review it before bringing it in.

The rate is your assumption. Actual returns can differ.
